# Privacy policy — Interval Pro

**App:** Interval Pro (`com.gsi85.intervalpro`), Android
**Developer:** `<YOUR NAME OR COMPANY>`
**Contact:** `<YOUR CONTACT EMAIL>`
**Effective date:** `<YYYY-MM-DD>`

**In one paragraph.** Interval Pro is an interval timer. It has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours,
and the app itself collects nothing about you. It shows a banner ad through Google's AdMob SDK, and that SDK
collects the data described in § 4. That is the whole of it.

---

## 1. What the app stores on your device

* Your settings (phase durations, sound cues) and the state of the current session, in the app's private
  storage on your phone.
* This never reaches us: there is no account, no sync and no server. Android's own optional app-backup
  setting is governed by your device, not by this app.
* **To delete it:** uninstall the app, or clear its data (Android *Settings → Apps → Interval Pro → Storage →
  Clear data*). Uninstalling removes everything the app stored.

## 2. What we collect

**Nothing.** We run no server, no account system, no analytics and no crash reporter. We never receive your
timer settings, your session history or any identifier for your device. We have no way to identify you.

## 3. What the app never accesses

No location (the app declares no location permission), no contacts, no camera, no microphone, no photos and
no files.

## 4. Advertising — Google AdMob

The banner is served by **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)**. To request, serve and measure it, Google's SDK collects
and shares with Google:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your device's **advertising ID (`AD_ID`)** and app-set ID | to select and measure the ad |
| Your **IP address**, which may be used to estimate general (approximate) location | ad selection |
| **App interactions** — app launch, taps and ad interactions | ad selection and measurement |
| **Diagnostics** — app/SDK performance such as launch time, hang rate and energy usage | analytics |

Purposes: advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention/security. This data is **not linked by us to your
identity**, and it is transmitted over **TLS**. We see only aggregate revenue and impression figures in
Google's reports — never your identifier.

Google's own documentation, which governs that data:

* How Google uses information from apps that use its services — <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
* Google Privacy Policy — <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
* Ad settings / opt out of personalised advertising — <https://adssettings.google.com>
* Reset or delete your advertising ID — Android *Settings → Privacy → Ads*

## 5. Your choice — consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Where consent is required, the first launch shows a **Google-certified consent form** (Google's User Messaging
Platform) asking whether you agree to personalised advertising. **No ad request is made before that choice is
resolved.** You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from inside the app: **long-press the timer
digits** to reopen the privacy options (that entry point is offered only where a form is required). Withdrawing
consent means you see no more personalised ads; the timer itself is unaffected either way.

## 6. Your rights

Because we hold **no personal data about you**, there is nothing on our side to retrieve, correct, export or
delete on request — that is why the app provides no deletion-request screen. The advertising data in § 4 is
processed by **Google** as an independent controller:

* to exercise rights over it (access, erasure, objection), use Google's controls linked in § 4, or contact
  Google through <https://policies.google.com/privacy>;
* you can **reset or delete your advertising ID** at any time in Android *Settings → Privacy → Ads*;
* if you believe your data has been handled unlawfully, you may lodge a complaint with your national
  data-protection authority;
* for any question about this policy, contact us at the address in § 8.

## 7. Children

Interval Pro is a general-audience utility, not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal
data from children. The advertising is not configured as child-directed.
